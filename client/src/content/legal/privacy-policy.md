**Last Updated: August 28, 2026**

(collectively, the **"Service"**) are operated by **NGF LLC** (**"NGF,"
"CoogsNation," "we," "our," or "us"**).

This Privacy Policy explains the categories of information we may
collect, how we may use it, how it may be disclosed, and choices that
may be available to you.

## 1. Scope

This Policy applies to the Service, including public pages, member
accounts, forums, chats, member profiles, Fan Commentary, media
features, Merlin, ecommerce features operated by NGF, and related
functionality.

Third-party sites, merchants, payment providers, ticketing services,
media providers, and other services have their own privacy practices.

## 4. Public Information

Content posted to public or community areas may be visible to other
users and, depending on the area and settings, may be indexed by
CoogsNation search, Merlin, or external search engines.

Public information may include a member handle, avatar, posts, public
profile information, public Fan Commentary, reactions, ranks, badges, or
other information the member chooses to make public.

Do not post information publicly that you do not want others to see or
potentially copy.

## 5. Age and Youth Privacy

Public CoogsNation content may be viewed by fans of any age unless a
particular feature is restricted.

Normal member accounts are intended for users age 13 and older.
CoogsNation does not knowingly permit a child under 13 to use a normal
member account unless NGF expressly offers a parent-authorized process
that complies with applicable law.

Where age information is used internally for eligibility or safety:

-   CoogsNation does not need to display a user's full birth date or
    exact age publicly;
-   NGF may apply additional safety restrictions to accounts known or
    reasonably believed to require youth protections;
-   NGF may restrict collection, visibility, private communications,
    purchases, location features, or other functionality where required
    by law or reasonably necessary for safety.

If NGF obtains actual knowledge that it is collecting personal
information through a normal account from a child under 13 without a
legally sufficient basis, NGF may restrict or close the account and
delete, retain, or process associated information as required by
applicable law.

## 6. Private Communications

Private messages and Private Email Relay communications are not publicly
visible by design.

However, they should not be understood as inaccessible to NGF in all
circumstances. NGF may process, store, review, preserve, or disclose
private communications as reasonably necessary to provide the feature,
maintain security, prevent fraud and abuse, investigate a user report,
protect users including minors, enforce the Terms or Posting & Community
Rules, or comply with legal obligations or lawful process.

NGF does not use private member communications as promotional content.

## 7. Merlin and AI Service Providers

Merlin may process a user's request and information the user is
authorized to access in order to provide requested functionality.

NGF may use third-party technology or AI service providers to support
Merlin or related features. Information sent to a provider should be
limited to what is reasonably necessary for the requested function and
handled according to NGF's agreements and applicable law.

Search and Merlin access remain subject to the permissions associated
with underlying content. Private content is not made public merely
because an authorized request is processed by Merlin.

## 12. Privacy Choices, Consent, and Preference Records

When the Service presents a privacy or cookie choice interface, users
may be offered controls such as **Accept All**, **Reject Optional**, and
**Modify Choices**.

Optional technologies subject to that choice are intended to remain
inactive until the applicable consent or permission has been obtained.
Selecting **Reject Optional** is intended to leave strictly necessary
technologies available while preventing optional categories controlled
by that interface from activating. Selecting **Modify Choices** allows
the user to choose among available optional categories.

Where affirmative consent is required, merely closing, ignoring,
hovering over, or continuing past the interface will not be treated as
affirmative consent.

NGF may maintain a privacy-preference record that includes a
pseudonymous or account-linked consent identifier, the policy or consent
version presented, the categories accepted or rejected, the date and
time of the choice, and subsequent changes. We use these records to
apply the user's preferences, document compliance, maintain security,
resolve disputes, and demonstrate the operation of our privacy controls.

Users may revisit available controls through **Privacy Choices** or a
substantially similar link or setting. Changing a preference applies
prospectively to technologies controlled by the preference system. A
change does not necessarily delete information lawfully collected before
the change; deletion and other privacy rights are handled under the
Privacy Rights section below.

NGF intends to configure newly added optional tracking or marketing
technologies as blocked by default until they have been classified and
placed under the appropriate consent or preference control.
